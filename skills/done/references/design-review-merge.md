# Design Review: Dispatch and Merge

Read this before dispatching the Design row's skills (capture widths, which screens each one sees, decks) and again once they return, before writing the Design row.

## Dispatch

- **`uiux`** grades the changed screens. First run `uiux`'s `scripts/check-render.mjs <url>` on each one: it takes the captures (320, 375, 768 and 1280 px, in each colour scheme) and measures what eyes on a screenshot keep missing (contrast on the real background, clipped or truncated text, overlaps, small tap targets, a missing pointer cursor, uneven sibling cards, card-in-card, sideways scroll, em dashes). Then open every capture and grade each against its sibling screens. A check-render FAIL or WARN is fixed or named as an exemption with its reason; it is never left as a finding.
- **`impeccable` critique** runs on the changed screens and starts its own two isolated reviewers. Hand it the capture set `uiux` made rather than a second one, after opening every capture to confirm it shows the changed state, not an error page or an empty list (20/11/2026: four reviewers across both skills were handed product captures that were all a 500 from an unrun migration and a listing emptied by a wrong fixture id, and spent their runs reporting the captures).
- **A screen behind a login is measured signed in, or the measurement is of the login page.** `check-render.mjs` and `uiux`'s `app-kit/scripts/inspect.mjs` take `--storage-state <file>` (the project's e2e saved login, which must be valid for the URL's host); `impeccable detect` takes `--cdp` against a signed-in browser. A `[]` or a clean report from a URL that redirected to `/login` describes the login page, so check the final URL before reporting the row (20/11/2026: both skills' rendered halves scanned a checkout's login redirect).
- **`taste`'s review path** runs on any screen a customer sees. An email or document a customer receives counts as one: on 15/11/2026 tenant emails were graded by `uiux` and `impeccable` only, a fourth `taste` omission. On a public page (landing, marketing, company site) that is `taste`'s `scripts/consistency.mjs <url>` and `scripts/copy-lint.mjs` (build-time checkers in 2.5.1 that also run on a page nobody built with `taste`); on a customer screen inside an app it is `uiux`'s app-screen inspector and lint (`app-kit/scripts/inspect.mjs --app` and `app-kit/scripts/lint.mjs --app`), since `taste` covers public pages only. **A shop's own storefront (a marketplace or grocery home, listing or product page of rails, deals and grids) takes the app-screen path too**, as `uiux` routes it: `consistency.mjs` scores it on marketing-page rules (hero fill, one CTA, headline copy) that would strip what makes it a shop (22/11/2026: 37 FAIL on a grocery home, nearly all of them those rules). The Design row says which path ran and why.
- **`emil-review-animations`** runs when the diff adds motion, decided from the diff, not session memory: search the added lines of `git diff HEAD` and of this session's commits for `transition`, `animation`, `@keyframes`, Tailwind `transition-`/`animate-`/`duration-` classes, a Chakra `transition=` prop, `animate`, `AnimatePresence`, `layoutId`, `drag=`/`dragConstraints`, `useSpring`, `vaul` or `Drawer`, and open each hit, since a word like `transition` can be a Laravel, state-machine or router name rather than motion. Pass the motion files and what each animation is for as the argument. It reads code, not captures, and judges against its own `STANDARDS.md` (curves, durations, origin, interruptibility, reduced motion). It runs whether or not the Design row does: a stylesheet no screen uses yet has motion to judge but nothing to capture. Its result is the Output's own Motion row, reading "no motion in diff" when the search finds nothing, so a skipped review and an unneeded one look different.

## Decks and rendered documents

A deck is graded from its rendered pages, not at widths. On a deck, `impeccable`'s detector reads only web markup, so its critique runs the design review alone, and the Design row says so. A render check for overflow is not this review: on 14/11/2026 a 31-slide deck passed every render check, and its owner then asked for the design skills because it looked generic.

## Merge

When the reviews that ran have returned (`taste`'s only on a screen a customer sees), merge them into one report with one score, since three separate scales give the reader no single verdict: `impeccable`'s Nielsen total is the starting score (N/M over the applicable maximum), each `uiux` or `taste` finding that bears on a heuristic lowers that heuristic and is named beside it, and an open `taste` inspector or `check-render.mjs` FAIL holds the whole score to 60% of the maximum. An `emil-review-animations` block (its blocking findings) lowers heuristic 1 (visibility of system status) for feedback and state motion, or heuristic 8 (aesthetic and minimalist design) for purposeless motion, and is named beside it. Findings are de-duplicated across the three (four with the motion review, which keeps its own Motion row too) and ranked by what the reader loses.

## Fix, then show

A design review here is a pass that improves the screen, not an audit. Every merged finding that does not change who can do or see something is fixed in this step, and check-render is re-run on the fixed screens. Only findings that change access, wording a customer is promised or a direction the user picked go to the user. Syafiq asked "Why does all other design skills only audit like that ?, is uiux alone enough ?" (15/11/2026) after critiques had reported findings that nothing built.

Then show the user the result: before and after captures of each changed screen at phone and desktop width, side by side, through the image gallery tool (`show_image` in syafiqkit) where it exists, otherwise as file paths in the reply; when the user may be on a phone or another device, through `SendUserFile` (`display: render`), since paths and the gallery do not reach them there. The Design row lists what was **changed** (finding, then fix), the merged score before and after, and anything left with its reason. **Tell: your Design row lists findings and no fixes.**
