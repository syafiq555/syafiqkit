# Bean Box subscription terms (made-up example)

## Billing
The customer is charged on the 1st of each month.
If the card fails, we retry after 3 days, then pause the box.
A paused box restarts when the customer updates the card.
When a charge fails the customer is emailed, and updates the card on the account page.

## Pricing
A box costs RM60.00 before discounts.
A first-month voucher takes RM10.00 off, funded by Bean Box.
Delivery costs RM8.00 per box.

## Roasters
Each roaster is paid 80% of the box price it supplied.
