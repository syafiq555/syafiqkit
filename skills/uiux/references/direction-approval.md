# Putting a new direction to approval

Before a new flow or page direction goes for approval, run both reviewers and fold their fixes in or put the open findings beside the question: `impeccable` `critique` on the direction, even unbuilt (from the agreed design and the files it extends; with nothing rendered its detector half has nothing to scan, so say so in the header rather than skip the critique), and, on any screen a customer sees, the app-screen inspector (`../app-kit/scripts/inspect.mjs --app` and `../app-kit/scripts/lint.mjs --app` on the current screen at its three widths, again with `--scheme dark` when the screen has a dark theme; on a public page, `taste`'s `scripts/consistency.mjs` and `copy-lint.mjs`). They catch different things: the critique judges the flow, the inspector what a first glance misses (contrast, foreign field labels, a control that reads as disabled). `uiux`'s routing (§ First decide which job this is in `../SKILL.md`) sends a whole job to either only when it is named, so `taste` was left out three times (2026-10-20, 2026-11-08, 2026-11-10: "how do u know taste is not ? probably got something can improve also"), and each time it found real defects once run. Use `AskUserQuestion` for genuine branches and inline prose for one direction to confirm. Mockups go in the reply above the question and again in the option `preview`, since either surface can fail to render (📖 `brainstorming` Step 4).
