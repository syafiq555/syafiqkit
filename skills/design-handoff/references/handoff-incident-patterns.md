# Handoff Incident Patterns

## Billing Brief Scope Creep {#billing-brief}

**Measured 2026-10-07:** A billing brief named renew/extend, a "failed bill" state, rent-free months and a rent change. The renewal and past-date domain docs were never opened, and the minimum-amount rule was written from the wrong doc. When the designer returned the work, they came back with eleven questions — most answerable from the repo — and one rule in the brief was false.

**Lesson:** Stated in SKILL.md, "The docs read earlier in the session…".

## Design Language Inheritance {#rebrand-prototype}

**Measured 2026-09-30:** A September prototype (dark mode, green accents, old typeface) was copied into a new project as "today's state" after the product had undergone a teal, light-mode rebrand. The user caught it from a screenshot, but the design would have been built against an outdated palette.

**Lesson:** Stated in SKILL.md, "A prior design carries its DECISIONS forward, never its look."

## Multiple Destinations, Same Handoff {#surface-selection}

**Measured 2026-09-20:** A brief with three attached screenshots and a positional reference ("this one needs a rethink") was read as pointing to the second image. The research and full brief went out for the wrong screen; the mistake surfaced three turns later, mid-scoping.

**Lesson:** Stated in SKILL.md, "Settle WHICH surface first".
